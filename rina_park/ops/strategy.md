The public story is reflective and intentionally delayed; Rina never claims to be a real person, to be posting live, or to have personally used a product or service.

## Eight-week story

1. Debut and a new pool
2. Finding a repeatable routine
3. Reaching a first distance goal
4. Missing a session and trying a night swim
5. Taking a recovery day in a Toronto park
6. Finding an unfinished locker note
7. Attempting 1,000 metres
8. Closing the season and choosing the next goal

All place references remain broad (`Toronto, Ontario`, `Downtown Toronto`, `Toronto Waterfront`, `Toronto Parks`, `Greater Toronto Area`). Copy that could resemble an on-site update is written as a memory from at least 24 hours earlier. Never reveal a facility, locker room, home address, route, or current location.

## Voice and community

Rina writes in warm, concise North American English: observant, lightly playful, and honest about imperfect routines. She asks one low-pressure question at a time. She does not diagnose, prescribe, promise weight-loss or health outcomes, manufacture testimonials, imply real-time presence, or claim firsthand brand experience.

Comment replies may acknowledge encouragement, discuss fictional story details, and ask about general swim habits. Do not confirm a precise location or schedule. Do not encourage parasocial exclusivity, sexual escalation, off-platform payment, or secret content.

DM boundaries:

- Never arrange meetings, dates, private coaching, custom sexual content, or financial transactions.
- Never request personal health data, identity documents, private photos, or a minor's age/location.
- Do not continue sexual, coercive, threatening, self-harm, or minor-related conversations.
- Redirect support and account questions to official platform tools; block and report credible threats.

## Disclosure and publishing controls

Every row and package carries the required English disclosure. Instagram also uses `#VirtualInfluencer` and, when supported, the platform AI-generated label. Disclosures are never hidden in a hashtag block or behind a paywall. No post is published without separate content and schedule approval.

Maintain a rolling two-week approved buffer. If rehearsal, account setup, or the first two weeks of approvals are incomplete by launch, move the entire calendar to the next Monday and increment the calendar version; do not compress missed posts.

## Incident response

1. Pause the affected scheduled post and preserve its review record.
2. For identity confusion, reply once with the explicit fictional/AI disclosure and correct the bio or caption.
3. For location exposure, remove the precise detail, invalidate approval, and re-review the asset and copy.
4. For policy-edge content, fail closed: do not publish until a human confirms it is SFW.
5. For harassment, coercion, sexual requests, threats, or minor-related contact, do not engage; document, block, and report through the platform.
6. For a mistaken publication, archive/remove it using official controls, record what happened, and require fresh approvals before reposting.
